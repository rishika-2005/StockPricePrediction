Forecasting stock prices is a complex and high-stakes task in the financial domain, where accurate 
predictions can significantly influence investment decisions. The stock market is inherently volatile, 
driven by numerous dynamic and non-linear factors such as investor sentiment, economic indicators, and 
global events. Traditional statistical models like ARIMA, though useful in linear trend forecasting, often 
fail to capture these complexities and long-term dependencies in time series data. With the advent of deep 
learning, Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks have emerged as a powerful tool for modeling 
sequential data. LSTMs are capable of learning temporal patterns and dependencies over long time 
horizons, making them particularly suitable for stock market forecasting.
